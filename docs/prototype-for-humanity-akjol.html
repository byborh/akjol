The problem — orientation is the last great inequality

Choosing what to study is among the most consequential decisions a young person makes — and one of the most unequal. A teenager whose parents are doctors or engineers inherits a network, private counselors and insider knowledge. A teenager from a working-class town or a rural area is handed a passive catalog and left to guess.

Existing tools make this worse, each in its own way:

Public catalogs

Onisep, Parcoursup — exhaustive but static. They list options; they never tell you what a choice means for someone in your situation.

Private platforms

Lead-generation engines that rank schools by who pays the most. Advice is structurally biased.

Generative AI chatbots

Fluent but unsourced — they fabricate admission rates, equivalences and outcomes with total confidence.

The human cost

Wasted years, debt, dropouts, forced reconversions — a decision worth tens of thousands of euros made with the least reliable information of one's life.

The solution — a router, not a catalog

AkJol (“White Path” in Kazakh) is an active study & career router. The student enters their starting point — current level, country, languages, budget, visa constraints — and AkJol computes every trajectory genuinely accessible to them, with explicit conditions, calibrated admission probabilities, the exact application procedure, and a Plan B if they are rejected.

“Students don't want to search a catalog — they want to be told honestly where each choice leads, including when it leads nowhere.”

Reverse routing

From a target job, trace back the study paths that reach it from this student's profile. Unique in French.

Radical honesty

Every figure shows its source and confidence interval. Every assumption is flagged. No orphan numbers.

Neutral by design

Organic results are never reordered by payment. No paid ranking, ever.

How it works

1 · PassportLevel, country, languages (CEFR), budget, visa — the single source of truth.
→
2 · Feasibility engineScores each path; separates verifiable conditions from estimates.
→
3 · TrajectoriesEvery accessible path, ranked, with admission odds + exact procedure.
→
4 · Plan BFallback routes, deadlines and opportunity cost if rejected.

Impact & alignment

AkJol runs on open public data, making it replicable in any country. The targeted impact is direct: reduce the orientation inequality driven by family social capital, cut avoidable dropouts and forced reconversions, and give a student from a forgotten town the same quality of guidance as one from a privileged district.

Equity

Same decision-grade guidance, independent of social capital.

Fewer wasted years

Surface dead-ends and Plan B before the choice is made.

Trust

Traceable sources counter AI-era misinformation.

Vision

AkJol becomes the francophone “life router”: orientation (15–25), reconversion (25–50), active retirement (50+). Starting with France & the Tech vertical, then expanding to Canada, Belgium, the UK and the US via open data connectors. If we succeed, fewer young people choose blindly — and a teenager's future depends on their ambition, not their postcode.

A student from a forgotten town gets the same quality of guidance as one from the wealthiest district. Give everyone their White Path.
